# PCV discovery results

Completed a source-only round-0 submission, then all three authorized rounds: timing at n=4,8,12,16,24,32; DrPerf with initial annotations at the same inputs; DrPerf with structural collector counts at n=2,6,20,40,64,96. All 18 supervised processes completed successfully and preserved output_columns=2*n. No optimization was made.

The principal discovery is repeated full outer-scope work while merging the n isolated table CTEs. The meaningful aggregate expressions are M*(P+J) and M*(M-1)/2, where M is the number of singular selected CTEs and P,J are the shared outer projection/join counts. At the individual _merge_expressions boundary, outer column volume, inner projection count, and reference-weighted copy volume explain the operations. The first timing round shows merge_ctes growing from 0.00461s at n=4 to 0.24658s at n=32, accounting for 0.247s of optimize's 0.477s at n=32. These are observed cProfile timings, not instruction-derived wall-time predictions.

Source locations: merge_subqueries.py merge_ctes line 93, _mergeable line 161, _merge_expressions line 416, _pop_cte line 558; scope.py Scope._collect; expressions/core.py Expression.set line 1076. M successful merges invalidate the outer scope cache, repeatedly traversing the same broad outer query. Removing a CTE also updates the entry list suffix; that suffix length is a direct PCV and sums quadratically when removing in order.

Scope collection itself depends on the number of scope-owned AST nodes and their argument slots, stopping at child scopes. The coarse first annotation (root projections, joins, CTEs) had 92% block-level residual. Structural counts reduced this to 59%; an offline weighted affine fit of saved per-state means using node and argument counts gives R2=0.999938 across 489 states. State-mean fit quality and DrPerf basic-block residual are distinct findings; substantial fine-grained unexplained work remains.

Additional source-based components are join dependency fanout squared, from repeated reference-list concatenation in optimize_joins.py, and AST/Identifier counts in quote_identifiers. Optimize_joins remains mostly linear over tested inputs: fanout copying is not demonstrated as the dominant cost.

Limits: the envelope permits only n_joins, so schema width, predicate size, CTE complexity, projections and joins could not be independently varied. All derived counts correlated with n must be interpreted with their source evidence. Round 3 added structural-count preparation outside child collector regions, increasing parent instruction counts; coefficients across annotation revisions must not be compared. Annotation wrappers have low lexical coverage and do not establish whole-source coverage. Reports contain unmodeled synchronization observations, but no semantic wait dependency is claimed. No workload replay or timing measurement is used as a PCV. Final claims and their limits are in hypothesis.json.
