# Timing discovery results

Final claims are in hypothesis.json and submitted as measurements/submissions/round-03.json. The source-only answer was submitted at round 00 before any measurements. No source or workload behavior was changed.

The key PCV is **total root-closure volume**, not just workflow node count. In caching.py:91 (add_keys), :101 (get_node_signature), and :137 (get_ordered_ancestry_internal), each root rebuilds its ancestry and signs every distinct ancestor. With N nodes, total visited/signed nodes T is N(N+1)/2 for chain and two-link DAG, but 2N-1 for star. At per-root entry r=int(node_id), closure size is r+1 for chain/DAG and 1 or 2 for star. Graph links and node IDs are already in the entry prompt; the closed forms do not replay signature generation.

The second PCV is **closure-weighted input and link volume**. Ancestry scans all inputs, and immediate signature creation at caching.py:109 scans inputs again. U counts input slots over all root closures and E counts linked slots. Chain has U=2T-N and E=T-N; star U=3N-2 and E=N-1; DAG U=3T-3N+1 and E=2T-3N+1. Thus the two-link graph has the same distinct ancestry volume as the chain, with extra input/link processing. Its redundant paths do not cause exponential traversal: order_mapping suppresses repeat visits within each root. At immediate-signature entry, local expressions are input dictionary length q and linked-value count e. Input sorting has bounded 1..3 keys in this workload.

The third PCV is **nested signature structure** for to_hashable at caching.py:54. H=N+3T+3U+3E counts recursive objects and S=N+T+U+E counts sequence containers. This gives H=6N^2+N for chain, 19N-12 for star, and 9N^2-8N+6 for DAG. At the outer conversion entry, H_key=1+sum(3+3q_v+3e_v) and S_key=1+sum(1+q_v+e_v), directly from the already-built immediate signature lists. These count objects without performing conversion or hashing. Scalar values and sequence containers have different processing costs.

Three timing rounds used 18 successful processes. Round 1 compared N=32 and 128 across all three shapes. Round 2 checked N=16,64,96 for chain/DAG. Round 3 repeated N=128 across all shapes twice. Every measured ancestry/immediate-signature call count matched T, and every recursive to_hashable call count matched H. profile_summary.csv records all measured counts and timings.

At N=128, the three runs per shape had add_keys cumulative cProfile times 0.5170..0.5193s chain, 0.01127..0.01141s star, and 0.6916..0.6961s DAG. Their ancestry counts were 8256,255,8256 and conversion counts 98432,2420,146438 respectively. Conversion dominated these regions: 0.4124..0.4141s chain, 0.00789..0.00798s star, and 0.5648..0.5679s DAG. Matched-N comparisons reject node-count-only explanations; matched-closure chain/DAG comparisons reveal the extra link/structure dependency.

Limits: these formulas are scoped to generated prompts, initial empty keys, unchanged=False, and no unique-ID signatures. Shape can be recovered from entry prompt links; at N=2 all formulas coincide. Arbitrary prompts would need distinct reachability and input-shape descriptors, rather than these family-specific closed forms. Timing coefficients are not identified: allocation, runtime state, possible garbage collection, and profiling overhead can vary. Smaller-size timings were not exactly affine in object counts. Process wall time includes large import/startup overhead and should not be confused with key-generation CPU-region timing. No instruction-count evidence was used, and no extrapolation beyond N<=128 was tested.
